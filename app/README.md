# La aldea de Santa

Responsive, buildless Spanish Christmas web app. Hosted assets are in `dist/`; `.openai/hosting.json` retains the existing Site identity. Preserve the Site's public audience.

- `data.js`: 54 greeting titles and their categories, price tiers and reference image rectangles. Prices: PEN 3 / USD 1 and PEN 5 / USD 2. The larger tier is five minutes or more; the shorter tier is one to three minutes. Calls and professional greetings currently use the base tier.
- `content.js`: 30 original six-page personalized story outlines with two narrative choices and ten coloring book definitions.
- `quiz-data.js`: 1000 unique questions in three difficulty levels across eleven categories. The 180 original Christmas questions are retained. Added subjects include mathematical reasoning (400), verbal reasoning (180), Bible (90), science (60), geography (40), history/art (30), and everyday/digital skills (20). `scripts/expand-quiz.py` reproduces the bank and checks unique stems, IDs and answer choices. Each round has ten questions; the 15-second real-clock deadline remains enabled by default. Certificates vary by score: 0–5 encouragement, 6 progress, 7–10 congratulations. At 7–10 a locally prepared, copyable message and the owner's unchanged WhatsApp link allow requesting a personalized greeting. Certificates remain downloadable as PNG/PDF and shareable through supported device menus.
- `app.js`: catalog, modal details, price-specific form and WhatsApp order composer. Direct contact uses the owner's custom w.app link. All WhatsApp buttons use the owner’s exact https://w.app/cubasai link in a new external tab with noopener/noreferrer. A persistent direct-contact button works without completing the order form. Orders are copied locally before opening the chat because the custom short link does not preserve arbitrary prefilled order text.
- `magic.js`: personalized PNG cards, A4 PDFs, QR cards, invitations, timed quiz with automatic participation certificates, story reader, speech synthesis and six-page coloring book exports.
- `assets/colorear/libro-N.webp`: ten 2-column, 3-row illustration sheets, one illustrated scene per PDF page.
- `vendor/`: jsPDF and QR Code Generator, distributed with their licenses.

All names and photos are processed in the visitor's browser. The app does not upload them to a server. Photos chosen for a paid greeting are previewed locally, and visitors attach them manually to their WhatsApp order. Nothing is sent until the visitor sends it in WhatsApp.

The supplied MP3 is a fixed Santa voice sample. Arbitrary story narration uses the browser's available Spanish speech voices, not a clone of the sample. Generated cards and stories use local templates and prepared photographic-style illustrations; they do not call a runtime generative AI service. QR cards encode the visitor-provided video URL. Catalog scenes are references; custom videos and any call or interactive delivery are coordinated separately with the owner.

No install or build is needed. Use Sites' managed preview and source/deployment workflow. Do not ship the temporary `__mobile-preview.html` QA harness.


## Tarjeta Navidad

The new section immediately follows the quiz and is linked next to Juega. `christmas-card-data.js` defines 25 distinct landscapes and Spanish wishes. `assets/navidad/diseno-01.webp` through `diseno-25.webp` are generated photographic-style fictional North Pole landscapes, with 25 lightweight thumbnails. The generation prompt set is in `christmas-image-prompts.json`.

`christmas-cards.js` creates a daily automatic card or a card with the visitor's name, with editable wishes. The real-clock countdown follows the visitor's local timezone, celebrates December 25 and rolls forward on December 26. The automatic design rotates daily; manual edits persist while the page stays open. The card refreshes while the section is visible. Downloads preserve their creation-time date and countdown; previously downloaded files cannot update themselves.

PNG exports are 1080×1920. MP4 exports are H.264/AAC, 720×1280 at 24 fps, 23.544 seconds, with the complete user-supplied `Village Christmas Lights.mp3` and a gentle fade at each end. Export runs locally in a lazy-loaded single-thread ffmpeg.wasm worker; no private data or names are uploaded. The unchanged WASM is split into two sub-25-MB static assets and reassembled into a Blob URL when requested. All scripts, WASM and music are hosted on the same origin. A progress indicator and cancel action are available, and the worker is terminated afterward to release memory. Share uses the native file menu when supported, otherwise it downloads the MP4 for manual attachment. No automatic posting or WhatsApp messages.

Vendor versions: @ffmpeg/ffmpeg 0.12.15 and @ffmpeg/core 0.12.10. License/source notices are in `vendor/ffmpeg`. The temporary `__cards-mobile.html` QA harness must not be published.

## La lista de Santa

`santa-list-data.js` defines 40 distinct reference-based photographic Santa scenes. `assets/listas/escena-01.webp` through `escena-40.webp` and matching `mini-*` thumbnails preserve the reference character and leave a golden stardust area in the upper sky. Supporting elves are young, clean-shaven adults. Source image prompts and generation paths are recorded in `santa-list-image-prompts.json`.

`santa-list.js` owns the expanded `#buenos` studio. Names are drawn exactly as typed (Unicode NFC) in thick, outlined golden lettering, rather than generated by an image model. The dropdown chooses “Lista de buenos” or “Lista de mal portados”. Both include supportive fantasy-card copy. A name, scene, optional message, voice and music can be selected. Downloads include 1080×1920 PNG, A4 PDF and H.264/AAC 720×1280 MP4 at 24 fps, exactly 15 seconds. The static photograph retains Santa's pose; the animated sky overlay reveals the name progressively from 4.8 to 9 seconds and holds it through the end. The browser processes names and exports locally, using the same lazy-loaded ffmpeg.wasm vendor files as Tarjeta Navidad.

The two owner-supplied MP3s are separate boy/girl voice choices. Both announce the good list; they do not dynamically pronounce the entered name. The bad-list choice therefore disables these incompatible voices and offers instrumental music or silence. Native file sharing is used where supported, with a download fallback. Export has progress, cancellation, and worker cleanup. Temporary mobile QA harnesses are not shipped.

## Music selection

`music-data.js` supplies a shared catalog of 11 original audio files: the retained Village Christmas Lights track and ten newly supplied MP3s, hosted unchanged in `assets/musica/`. Distinct alternate recordings retain separate “Versión 2” labels. Both Tarjeta Navidad and La lista de Santa offer music selectors and native audio previews. Choosing a different track invalidates any cached video so the next download includes that selection. Tarjeta Navidad exports the full selected track (23.832–26.424 seconds for the new tracks), or a silent 15-second video. La lista de Santa retains its 15-second name animation and mixes the selected music with the existing optional boy/girl announcement; its music checkbox still allows voice-only or silent videos. All exports and audio mixing run locally in the visitor's browser.

## Pages and moving Santa video

`pages.js` groups the existing sections into accessible hash-addressed views. The first view shows the enlarged brand, countdown and navigation without prices. `#magia` is the second-page activity hub, linking all studios, greetings and help. `#catalogo` and `#pedido` form one greeting page; the paid catalog and original prices are retained there. Navigation includes Lista de los buenos directly next to Saludos. Existing deep links still resolve.

`#lista-buenos` offers five of the owner's uploaded motion videos unchanged: `santa-polvo.mp4`, `santa-bolsita.mp4`, `santa-destellos.mp4`, `santa-luna.mp4`, and `santa-guirnaldas.mp4` in `assets/lista-video/`. A dropdown and accessible thumbnail buttons select the scene; preview, PNG cover, and MP4 export all use that selection. Changing a scene stops playback and clears previously generated downloads/sharing data; filenames include the scene ID. Poster loads are guarded against rapid selection changes. `list-video.js` combines it with the existing fixed male/female announcements, trimming only their final silence to 4.2/3.9-second WAVs. Gold star-letter animation begins at the selected name’s speech onset, sampled at 24 fps. The moving-video studio now looks up the chosen complete name in `name-audio-data.js` and appends only its isolated owner-supplied recording after the fixed announcement. `scripts/name-audio-manifest.json` records the twelve source files and exact per-name cut boundaries. No speech synthesis is used. Accent/case normalization and a short list of equivalent spelling aliases preserve the typed display name; there is no fuzzy or partial-name matching. Unknown names are explicitly reported and cannot generate a falsely named spoken video; their PNG cover remains available. The original clip plays once; its final frame is held to complete a 10-second, 720×1280 H.264/AAC MP4. The user can preview, export, cancel, save a PNG cover and select any existing music track.

`video-sharing.js` provides TikTok, WhatsApp and other-network actions for all three video studios. Supported devices invoke native file sharing and let the user select an installed app. Unsupported browsers download the actual MP4 and explain manual attachment. Buttons never claim automatic posting, open fake upload URLs, or send messages without the visitor's action. No TikTok OAuth developer client is configured. Changing inputs clears the ready-to-share file. All name and video processing remains local.

The moving-video studio uses a shared 10-second duration for all five scenes, previews, voices/music mixing and MP4 output (240 frames at 24 fps). Names use a bold, automatically fitted font up to 300px, with a 1.6-second writing reveal after the selected announcement. Individual golden particles flow into the writing edge; text-shaped fine glitter and star glints blend the name with the uploaded footage. The original 7.93-second clips are preserved; the final camera frame holds while name sparkles continue until second 10. Cover images use each clip’s sky-filled 6-second frame. Other studios retain their own durations.

## Recorded name lookup

The catalog contains 864 distinct names, backed by 968 isolated WAV recordings, each mono PCM16 at 44.1 kHz. The browser concatenates the chosen 4.2/3.9-second announcement, a 60ms natural gap, and one selected name in a single WAV buffer. Preview and FFmpeg export use exactly that same combined audio. The gold writing starts at `announcement.end + 0.06 + recording.lead`, within the 24fps frame interval; its duration follows the selected recording. The full original lists are never played or exported. The UI offers name suggestions, an exact-match status, a preview of the isolated name, and the combined phrase/name preview. A recognized name automatically selects the corresponding boy/girl announcement; the selector remains available. All processing stays local to the visitor’s browser. Source audio is only cut at silent boundaries; names retain the owner’s Santa voice.

The second import adds 200 recordings from six additional owner-supplied lists. Elena, Nicole, and Emanuel already existed, so their new clips are retained as alternates without duplicate suggestions. The resulting catalog has 398 names; `scripts/name-audio-batch2-report.json` records the counts. Exact spelling, case-insensitive with NFC normalization, takes precedence over accent-insensitive fallback: Jordan and Jordán select their distinct recordings; names such as Jose still find José. Ambiguous fallback matches are rejected. Displayed names always preserve the visitor’s input, including Jazmin without an accent. Existing 10-second video timing, golden writing, five scenes, and music/share controls are retained.

## Continuous questions and video editor

`quiz-continuous.js` preserves the reviewed 1000-question bank and tracks seen IDs locally. Continuous mode provides ten-question blocks and a certificate per block. Once the selected bank is exhausted it creates new arithmetic, reading word problems and measurement variants with exact BigInt answers and a persistent counter. This is procedural practice, not an infinitely researched facts bank.

`#editor` supports local videos or ready creations from the three studios, start/end trimming (up to ten minutes), vertical/square/horizontal/original proportions, static text, original sound volume, eleven music tracks or a local audio file, and publication copy. It exports a H.264/AAC MP4 copy using the existing FFmpeg worker, with progress, cancellation, playback, download and native file sharing. All files stay in the browser. Users complete their posts in their chosen app; automatic TikTok posting requires a separately registered and authorized Content Posting API integration.

The third name import adds 567 recordings from sixteen owner-supplied lists: 466 new names and 101 alternates, for 864 distinct names and 968 recordings. Spellings use the owner's `listas_1000_santa_actualizadas.txt`. The manifest records exact source boundaries and the batch report records counts. The 398 previous names, five scenes, 10-second duration, synchronized gold writing and 11 music choices are retained. Suggestions also include spelling aliases while display preserves typed input.
